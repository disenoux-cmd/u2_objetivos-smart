# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Selected for direct, dependency-free deployment to GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages.

## Users

EcoS participating in the self-directed Gestion y Cultura de Aula course. They need to understand and apply SMART learning objectives autonomously from desktop, tablet, or mobile devices.

## Product Purpose

Build understanding of learning objectives, distinguish knowledge from skill, unpack the SMART criteria, analyze contextualized examples, and connect precise planning with protecting effective classroom learning time. Success means that the learner can recognize the strengths and weaknesses of a learning objective and identify observable evidence.

## Positioning

The resource makes SMART criteria visible inside authentic classroom objectives instead of presenting the acronym only as a definition to memorize.

## Operating Context

The RED is embedded or linked from a self-directed Moodle course and can be hosted as a static site. The learner follows a guided but flexible route, can revisit previous concepts, receives immediate feedback, and closes by reflecting on what they learned and how they will apply it in the classroom.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Interactive navigation inspired by Genially without depending on Genially.
- No textareas or text inputs because learner responses are not persisted or reviewed in this resource.
- Immediate formative feedback for selected-response activities.
- No unit name, level, or duration shown in the learner-facing interface.
- Works without a backend and without runtime API keys.

## Brand Commitments

Use the Enseña por Colombia logo assets in `docs/`, an inclusive, close, practical, pedagogical, and inspiring Spanish voice, and a professional learning-oriented presentation.

## Evidence on Hand

- Detailed content and interaction specification: `docs/RED.md`.
- Course instructional design: `docs/Diseño Instruccional- GCA.md`.
- Institutional logos: `docs/excolombia.png` and `docs/exclombia-blanco.png`.
- No testimonials, performance data, or learner analytics are available and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make each concept actionable through exploration and immediate feedback.
- Protect learner autonomy with clear orientation, visible progress, and easy return paths.
- Prefer observable classroom evidence over abstract explanation.
- Keep interactions meaningful, concise, and usable without persistence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive access across mobile, tablet, and desktop; high contrast; keyboard-operable controls; visible focus states; reduced-motion support; semantic structure; and inclusive Spanish language.
